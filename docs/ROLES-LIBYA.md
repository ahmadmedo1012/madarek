# ROLES-LIBYA — the real Libyan education roles (evidence-backed)

Purpose: ground Madarek's role/permission model in the ACTUAL structures of
Libyan education — universities under وزارة التعليم العالي والبحث العلمي
(Ministry of Higher Education & Scientific Research), schools under وزارة
التربية والتعليم (Ministry of Education). Produced for R353 lane E.
Method: primary-text retrieval (legislation mirrors on ju.edu.ly, university
org/job-description PDFs, live university and ministry pages); blocked or
empty sources disclosed honestly.
Date: 2026-11-14 (R353, E1-RESEARCH).

**Naming note.** Decisions 22/2008 & 501/2010 predate the 2011 rename; their
«أمين اللجنة الشعبية…» titles are today's رئيس الجامعة / عميد الكلية /
رئيس القسم (the published texts carry an explicit 2011
Constitutional-Declaration adaptation note, corroborated by current university
sites: Sebha, Gharyan, Benghazi).

### How مكتب القبول والتسجيل works

- **Admission (القبول):** secondary-certificate holders apply; files land at the
  college's قسم القبول والتسجيل which vets and completes procedures, then
  distributes student lists to scientific departments.
- **Registration (التسجيل):** the unit keeps سجلات قيد الطلاب — the
  student's الرقم الجامعي (رقم القيد) is issued at FIRST registration and
  follows them on every grade sheet; the same unit issues البطاقة الجامعية,
  handles transfers (انتقال), enrollment freeze/resume (وقف القيد/إعادته),
  and enrollment certificates.
- **Graduates:** قسم الخريجين holds final results, prepares
  transcripts/graduation confirmations, releases them after approvals.
- **Exam seat numbers (أرقام الجلوس)** and eligibility lists are prepared by
  قسم الدراسة والامتحانات — SEPARATE from القبول والتسجيل.

### Final exam committees (لجنة الامتحانات النهائية)

- Formation: وكيل الكلية للشؤون العلمية supervises final exams and forms
  their committees in coordination with the scientific departments.
- Composition (501/2010): several faculty members + مسجل الكلية (or institute
  registrar) + رئيس قسم الدراسة والامتحانات + staff «of proven competence
  and integrity».
- Workflow: invigilator reports violations → committee chair → Dean; graded
  results entered in sheets signed by writer, system-enterer and committee
  chair → Dean approval; transcripts signed by dept + مسجل الكلية + Dean;
  graduation confirmation by مسجل + Dean; degree certificate by المسجل العام
  + Dean + رئيس الجامعة.

## 3. Platform mapping (what Madarek implements NOW)

Madarek today is a **higher-education** platform (institutions → faculties →
departments → courses/offerings → sections, with exams, lectures, attendance,
grades, research, announcements). Schools are a LATER phase. The mapping
below uses the current product's real seams (from the E3 permission
inventory: the Capability layer, `scopeFacultyId`, `TeacherProfile.position`)
plus the conservative rule for unverified roles.

| Libyan role | In scope now | Platform seat | Conservative permission set |
|---|---|---|---|
| المسجل العام (registrar general) | **YES — now** | `Role.ADMIN` unscoped (already branded «المسجل العام» in the UI) | Full student-records custody: user lifecycle, bulk import, enrollments, directory/PII, reset links, catalogue recording, imports. NOT exam authoring, NOT quality. |
| مسجل الكلية (college registrar) | **YES — now** | `Role.ADMIN` + `scopeFacultyId` (exists; gets a UI + first-class label) | Same as registrar general, pinned to own faculty (the +fac scoping that already exists on user/enrollment/directory routes). |
| قسم القبول والتسجيل staff | later | — | Registrar general/college registrar covers the workflow; a dedicated clerk seat adds nothing until multi-cllege scale. |
| مراقب/قسم الدراسة والامتحانات (exams controller) | **YES — now** | NEW `StaffRole.EXAMS_CONTROLLER` (capability `EXAMS_MODERATE` split out of QUALITY) | Exam-cycle owner: moderation queue, template approval, eligibility/seat lists (when exam logistics ship), results workflow. NOT question authoring (teachers), NOT grades of coursework. |
| لجنة الامتحانات (exam committee) | later (workflow) | — | A future approval workflow (chair signs before dean); today moderation covers the control point. |
| المعلم / عضو هيئة التدريس (all ranks incl. معيد) | **YES — now** | `Role.TEACHER` (ranks stay profile data, not permissions) | Own-offerings teaching: materials, assignments, attendance, grades for own sections, question authoring, live sessions. |
| رئيس القسم (department head) | **YES — now** | `TeacherProfile.position = DEPARTMENT_HEAD` becomes LOAD-BEARING: department-scoped read of its offerings/rosters + curriculum propose/approve within department | Department scoping (read) + curriculum approval within own department. Conservative: read + approve, never cross-department. |
| وكيل الكلية (vice dean) | **YES — now** | `position = ASSOCIATE_DEAN` load-bearing: exam-cycle supervision within faculty (approves moderation outcomes), deprivation/graduates lists | Faculty-scoped exam-cycle oversight. Conservative: everything the dean can do EXCEPT final approvals. |
| عميد الكلية (dean) | **YES — now** | `position = DEAN` load-bearing: faculty-scoped oversight + final approvals (results, transcripts workflow) + grants system permissions within faculty | Faculty-scoped read-everything + approval actions. NOT platform administration (no user creation outside faculty, no imports). |
| رئيس الجامعة / وكيل الجامعة | later | — | Platform is single-institution today; university tier = the OWNER console at most. Conservative: none until multi-college data exists. |
| المرشد الأكاديمي (academic advisor) | later (unverified legally) | — | Conservative: none. The AI assistant's mastery-aware mode already covers the guidance use case without a role. |
| مكتب الجودة (quality office) | **YES — now** | `Role.QUALITY` (minus EXAMS_MODERATE which moves to exams controller) | Quality views: course quality, engagement, curriculum review, professor eval, reports. Read-only by default. |
| الموظف الإداري (admin staff) | later | — | Conservative: none (registrar seats cover the platform's admin work). |
| الطالب (student) | **YES — now** | `Role.STUDENT` | Own data only: enrollments, grades, materials of enrolled offerings, exams, research. |
| ولي الأمر (guardian) | later (universities: no legal seat) | — | Schools phase only. |
| مدير النظام (system admin) | **YES — now** | `Role.OWNER` (platform owner) | Platform operations: institutions, settings, flags, system health. Not a university role. |

**Decision (E2/E3 input):** `Role` stays the 5-value authN enum; the exact
Libyan taxonomy rides (a) the existing `Capability` layer for actions,
(b) `TeacherProfile.position` (DEAN/ASSOCIATE_DEAN/DEPARTMENT_HEAD) made
load-bearing with faculty/department scoping, (c) a NEW `StaffRole`
axis for non-teaching officers (registrar general, college registrar,
exams controller, quality) replacing the ad-hoc ADMIN folding, (d)
`scopeFacultyId` surfaced in the UI. Existing ADMINs map conservatively
(see the E2 migration) and are listed for the owner.

## 5. Open uncertainties

1. **Law 4/2020 (الجامعات)** supersedes parts of 501/2010 but the only copy
   found is a scanned PDF; its articles remain unverified.
2. **وكيل الجامعة للشؤون الإدارية والمالية**: reported at some universities
   (Tripoli, Benghazi) but not confirmed in fetched texts — only وكيل
   الشؤون العلمية is verified.
3. **المرشد الأكاديمي** (universities): no legal basis found; if implemented,
   model it as a department-level optional role.
4. **School roles** (مدير/وكيل المدرسة، المرشد التربوي، رئيس القسم، مراقب
   الدراسة والامتحانات): both school searches returned no usable duties
   pages; conservative sets given — verify against MoE school-administration
   regulations in the schools phase.
5. **Appointment mechanisms** (who appoints عميد/رئيس الجامعة) and the
   **national electronic admission system** (القبول الإلكتروني) were not
   verified in this pass.
6. **Reachability:** mohe.gov.ly (no DNS from the sandbox) and uot.edu.ly
   (Cloudflare block) could not be inspected directly; su.edu.ly blocked by
   firewall (snippet only) — evidence for those institutions is second-hand.
7. **Pre-2011 title mapping** is corroborated by current university sites but
   no single fetched document states the renaming formally.
8. Each university issues its own لائحة داخلية / نظام دراسة وامتحانات;
   details vary per institution and were not collected.
